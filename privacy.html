Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version: we collect only the small amount of information an order or an email signup actually needs, we use it only to deliver what you asked for, and we never sell your personal data. Here is the full picture.

What we collect, and who collects it

  • When you buy: your name, email address and order details are collected at checkout by Polar, our payment provider and Merchant of Record, with payments processed via Stripe. We receive your name, email and what you ordered so we can deliver your product and support you.
  • When you sign up for emails: your name and email address are collected by Brevo, our email provider — for example when you request the 5 free captions or join our newsletter. Signups use double opt-in, so your address is only added once you confirm from your own inbox.

Your payment details

We never see, receive or store your card number or other payment credentials. All payment data is handled directly by Polar and Stripe under their own security and privacy standards.

How we use your email address

  • To deliver your purchases and your free captions.
  • To send order-related messages, such as receipts, delivery links and subscription notices.
  • To send occasional product news and new caption ideas — only if you signed up for emails, and every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link that works immediately.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

Cookies on this site

This store is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no tracking cookies of its own and runs no advertising trackers. Checkout and email signup pages are provided by Polar, Stripe and Brevo, and those services may use cookies that are necessary for their checkout and forms to work securely.

How long we keep your data

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax and support purposes. Email subscribers stay on our list until they unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.

Your choices and data requests

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email. To see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, just email us at hello@hellobumpandbloom.com and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at hello@hellobumpandbloom.com.
