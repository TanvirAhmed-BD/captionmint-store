Refund Policy

Simple, fair, and in writing — just how it should be.

When you get a full refund — no questions, no fuss

If any of these happen, we will refund you in full within 7 days of your purchase:

  • The file is defective or corrupted and will not open or work.
  • You received the wrong product.
  • Your download or access link never arrived — and we could not fix it after you contacted us.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase by mistake.

Just email us at hello@hellobumpandbloom.com from the email address you used for the order, tell us what went wrong, and we will sort it out. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, processed via Polar — depending on your bank, the money can take a few business days to appear after it is issued.

Change of mind

Because every product here is an instantly downloadable digital good, a change of mind after a successful download is not refundable — once the files are with you, they cannot be returned. If you are unsure whether a pack is right for you, start with the 5 free captions first, or email us before buying and we will happily answer your questions.

Caption Club subscription

You can cancel Caption Club at any time. Cancellation stops all future renewals, and your current paid period simply stays active until it ends — you keep everything delivered during that period. The refund situations listed above apply to a subscription payment in the same way.

Anything else?

If something about your order feels wrong and it is not covered above, email us anyway at hello@hellobumpandbloom.com. We are a small studio and we would rather make it right than leave a customer unhappy.
